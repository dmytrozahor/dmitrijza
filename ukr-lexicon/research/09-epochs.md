Notes:
- The chapter title gives the name: «57. ПЕРЕХІД (IV) ВІД СЕРЕДНЬОУКРАЇНСЬКОЇ ДО ПІЗНЬОСЕРЕДНЬОУКРАЇНСЬКОЇ ТА НОВІТНЬОЇ ДОБИ» [Q].

Do **not** use it for labels.
- WIKI «Історія української мови», §Періодизація, summarises Shevelov differently, e.g. [Q\*] «середньоукраїнський період середина XVI — перші роки XVIII ст.», «сучасний період від останніх років XVIII ст. до сьогодні». The primary text (1575/1720/1818) takes precedence. The wiki rounding explains the user's "mid-XVI–XVIII" and "XIX c.–" figures.

[P] Notes on the mapping:
- `wl.rus` start 988 (Christianization) and end 1349 are our convention. ENC um102 says "14 ст." for Ruthenian, and WIKI says "до сер. XIV ст.". Flag this row `dates_conventional=true`.
- `wl.contemporary` sits inside `wl.new`. It is an overlay, not an exclusive epoch.
- Gaps and overlaps between schemes are expected. Never merge the two schemes. Store `epoch_shev` and `epoch_wl` separately.

### Assignment algorithm [P]

1. **Normalise the attestation date** to an inclusive year range [a, b]. Use the *composition* date. Store the manuscript/copy date separately as `copy_date`, because epoch reflects the language of the text.
   - "XVI ст." → 1501–1600.
   - "поч./сер./кін. XVI ст." → 1501–1633 / 1634–1666 / 1667–1700 (thirds).
   - "1-а/2-а пол." → halves.
   - "бл. 1600" → 1590–1610.
   - "до 1646" → (1646−50)–1646.
   - "1595-1600" → as given.
2. For each scheme, compute overlap `o_e = |[a,b] ∩ [start_e − fuzz, end_e + fuzz]|` and the share `s_e = o_e / (b − a + 1)`.
3. **Exact:** if [a,b] lies inside one epoch's *core* interval (without fuzz), assign it with `epoch_confidence=high`.
4. **Edge:** if the best epoch has s ≥ 0.8 (fuzz included), assign it with `epoch_confidence=medium` and `boundary_note` naming the neighbouring epoch.
5. **Span:** otherwise assign the ordered list of all epochs with s > 0.1 and `epoch_confidence=low`. Do not average.
6. **Overlays:** `wl.ruthenian.chancery` and `wl.contemporary` are extra tags added on top of the exclusive epoch.
7. Always store `epoch_source` (SHEV ch.x.y or ENC umNN URL) and keep the quote ID from this file. The UI shows Shevelov's period by default for phonological forms and `wl.*` for lexical/orthographic attestations.

Example: Пересопницьке Євангеліє, 1556–1561 → `shev.early_middle` (high), `wl.ruthenian` (high). «Енеїда», 1798 → `shev.late_middle` (high; Shevelov lists Котляревський 1798–1822 among the period's monuments in 57.4), `wl.new` (high). This disagreement is intended.
